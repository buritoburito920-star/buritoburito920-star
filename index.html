Features

Everything you need to leave a print unattended.

AI failure detection

A vision model scores every camera check for spaghetti and highlights the suspect area on the live feed.

You choose the response

Pause, cancel, or alert only. Require several detections in a row so one bad frame never stops a good print.

Live camera view

Watch the build plate from any device on your network, with print progress, layers and ETA alongside.

Discord alerts

Get notified when a print starts, finishes, pauses or cancels, with first-frame, last-frame and periodic pictures.

Automatic time-lapses

Every print is recorded and ready to download from the dashboard. Set the interval and how many to keep.

Console and temperatures

Send G-code, set nozzle and bed targets, and follow live temperature and certainty graphs without leaving the page.

How it works

Three steps, then it watches for you.

Connect

Enter your printer's IP address. Guardian talks to the AD5M over your local network.

Watch

The camera is checked continuously. Suspected defects are highlighted and counted as hits.

Act

Once the hit count is reached, Guardian pauses, cancels or alerts you, depending on your setting.

Tuning

Decide how cautious it should be.

More required hits means fewer false alarms but a slower reaction. Try it. Each AI check takes roughly five seconds.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which printers does it support?

Guardian is built for the FlashForge Adventurer 5M and talks to it over the local network on port 8899.

Do I need to modify my printer?

No. There are zero hardware mods. You only need a camera pointed at the build plate.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes. That's why you can require several detections in a row, raise the minimum certainty, or choose alert only and make the call yourself.

Where do I get help or share feedback?

Join the Discord community or open an issue on GitHub. Guardian is in active beta, so feedback shapes what comes next.
